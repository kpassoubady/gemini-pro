# Day 2 Kahoot 4: Gemini Notebook Foundations and Sourcing

**Placement:** After Sessions 13 and 15, before Session 17
**Scope:** Gemini Notebook foundation, data sourcing, and source management

## Q1 (MCQ)

**Question:** What is Gemini Notebook designed to minimize?

A. Hallucinations through source grounding
B. Source selection through automatic expansion
C. Review time through unsourced summaries
D. Collaboration limits through isolated chats

**Correct Answer:** A

## Q2 (MCQ)

**Question:** What is the main role of a Notebook project space?

A. To organize sources, instructions, chats, and notes
B. To draft presentations without any source set
C. To keep only the latest source version
D. To separate every source into its own chat

**Correct Answer:** A

## Q3 (True/False)

**Question:** A Notebook can ingest PDFs, Google Docs, web articles, and raw notes.

A. True
B. False

**Correct Answer:** A

## Q4 (MCQ)

**Question:** What does selecting and deselecting sources control?

A. The context available to the response
B. The source file’s original author
C. The order of the notebook’s saved notes
D. The permissions on the learner’s account

**Correct Answer:** A

## Q5 (True/False)

**Question:** Uploading more sources always makes a Notebook answer more relevant.

A. True
B. False

**Correct Answer:** B

## Q6 (MCQ)

**Question:** Why inspect sources before asking cross-source questions?

A. To confirm relevance, quality, and coverage
B. To prioritize the newest source automatically
C. To make conflicting sources agree
D. To limit the Notebook to one source

**Correct Answer:** A

## Q7 (MCQ)

**Question:** Which source set best demonstrates diverse Notebook inputs?

A. A PDF report, web article, Google Doc, and raw notes

## Q8 (True/False)

**Question:** Source management lets learners focus a response on selected inputs.

B. False

## Q9 (MCQ)

**Question:** What does bidirectional syncing help keep current?

A. Live documents connected to the Notebook

**Question:** A Notebook is a persistent research workspace, not just a one-time chat upload.

**Question:** What should a learner do when a source is irrelevant to the current question?
