# Demo: Turn Verified Notes into Study Aids

## Purpose

Create a manager briefing, study guide, and quiz from verified notes while checking that generated answers remain grounded.

## Preparation

Use the checked rows from Demo 07. Save the effective date, enrollment deadline, field-operations exception, and unresolved rollout-method question as notes with their citations.

## Delivery

1. Select only the verified notes and combine them into a manager briefing.
2. Confirm that the unresolved rollout method remains a question, not a requirement.
3. Generate a study guide for managers with key questions and a glossary.
4. Generate five medium-difficulty quiz questions focused on policy application.
5. Open the custom prompt, then verify one answer key against the original policy.

## Acceptance check

- The study guide separates requirements from recommendations.
- Every date and exception maps to an original source passage.
- The quiz has one defensible answer per question.
- No generated note is treated as primary evidence.
- Export permissions are reviewed separately from notebook access.

## Offline fallback

A quiz asks, “Which phased rollout method does the policy require?” The correct review outcome is to delete or rewrite the question because the policy does not establish a phased rollout method.

## Takeaway

Generate learning artifacts from verified notes, then check the artifact against primary sources rather than trusting the intermediate synthesis.
