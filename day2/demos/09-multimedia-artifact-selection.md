# Demo: Choose and Review a Multimedia Artifact

## Purpose

Choose between an infographic and Video Overview for a policy timeline, then review a prepared visual plan for factual and accessibility defects.

## Preparation

Open the verified `Remote Access Policy Review` notebook. Select only the policy and implementation guide. Keep a prepared screenshot or storyboard fallback because video generation can take more than 30 minutes.

## Artifact brief

```text
Create a landscape visual for managers. Show the 10 October enrollment deadline, the 15 October effective date, and the approved field-operations exception that lasts until regional security review. Keep the exception visually distinct from the main timeline. Use only the selected policy and implementation guide. Do not invent rollout phases, adoption metrics, employee counts, or approval status.
```

## Delivery

1. Ask learners whether a one-screen infographic or Cinematic Video best fits three milestones.
2. Generate the infographic or inspect the prepared output.
3. Check every date, label, arrow, and exception against source passages.
4. Verify contrast, reading order, and an accessible text equivalent.
5. Explain when an Explainer or Cinematic Video would justify its added complexity.

## Offline fallback defects

The prepared visual places enrollment after the effective date, omits “approved” from the field exception, and labels the public article’s pilot recommendation as mandatory. Learners must find all three defects.

## Takeaway

Choose the smallest artifact that serves the communication need, then review visual meaning as carefully as written claims.
