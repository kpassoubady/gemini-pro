Workshop: Mastering Gemini Notebook & AI-Powered Research
Target Audience: Students, researchers, or professionals looking to optimize their information synthesis.
Format: 4 hours total (Divided into 4 modules with brief transitions).

Hour 1: Foundation and Data Sourcing
Focus: Workspace setup, understanding the architecture, and ingesting information.

10 min - Introduction to Gemini Notebook: Explain the core concept (formerly NotebookLM integration) as a centralized research and study workspace. Differentiate it from standard AI chat by emphasizing source-grounding (minimizing hallucinations). Note the capacity: up to 500 notebooks and 300 sources per notebook.

15 min - Creating the Workspace: Have participants create their first project space.

20 min - Hands-on Sourcing: Instruct learners to upload a diverse set of materials on a single topic (e.g., a PDF report, a Google Doc, a pasted web article, and raw notes).

15 min - Source Management: Walk through how to select/deselect specific sources to narrow the AI's focus, and how bidirectional source syncing keeps data updated.

Hour 2: Synthesis and Active Interrogation
Focus: Chatting with documents, extracting insights, and generating study materials.

15 min - Querying the Data: Teach prompt engineering specific to Notebook. Have participants ask cross-document questions (e.g., "Synthesize the main arguments from the PDF and compare them to the web article").

25 min - Generating Study Aids:

Have participants use the built-in tools to instantly generate a digital study guide.

Create a set of practice quizzes and interactive flashcards based on their uploaded materials.

20 min - Note Management: Practice extracting the best AI answers, saving them as permanent notes, and organizing them within the project space.

Hour 3: Multimedia Generation
Focus: Transforming text into engaging audio and visual formats.

15 min - Infographics & Cinematic Video: Guide participants in generating an infographic or a Cinematic Video Overview that visually summarizes a complex concept from their notebook.

25 min - Audio Overviews (Interactive Podcast):

Demonstrate how to generate a two-host AI podcast discussing the source material.

Practical Exercise: Have participants generate an Audio Overview and practice using the "live interruption" feature to steer the AI hosts' conversation in real-time.

20 min - Review & Export: Discuss how to export these multimedia assets for presentations or external sharing.

Hour 4: Capstone Project & Workflow Integration
Focus: End-to-end application and tying the tools into a daily workflow.

10 min - Capstone Briefing: Instruct participants to pick a fresh, real-world topic relevant to their work or studies.

35 min - Independent Build: Participants must independently create a new notebook, source the data, generate a study guide, create one multimedia asset (audio or visual), and save key analytical notes.

15 min - Showcase & Q&A: Have a few volunteers share their Audio Overviews or insights. Wrap up with best practices for maintaining these notebooks over time.
