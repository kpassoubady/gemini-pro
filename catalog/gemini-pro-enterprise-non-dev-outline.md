# Gemini Enterprise for Organizations (2 Days)

**Duration**: 2 days

**Audience**: Professionals who use Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, YouTube, and Gemini in their daily work

**Track**: Practical generative artificial intelligence for Google Workspace users

**Prerequisites**: A Google account with access to Gemini and Google Workspace; access to a few sample emails, documents, or personal tasks to use during independent labs.

## Course Overview

This course is designed for non-developers who want to use Gemini to conquer their everyday tasks while learning advanced, unexpected prompting techniques. Moving beyond basic "hello world" prompts, participants will discover how to reverse-engineer images, generate master prompts, synthesize deep insights from videos, and manage their inbox and calendars with precision. The course covers everything from drafting smart documents to building a centralized research hub in Gemini Notebook. Every topic is modular: participants learn an advanced technique during a demonstration and immediately apply it in an independent lab.

## Main Topics Covered

- Mastering the Inbox with Gmail (summarization, tone-matching, finding action items)
- Smart Document Creation in Google Docs (iterative drafting, summarizing long PDFs)
- Everyday Data Assistant in Google Sheets (unstructured data extraction, simple trackers)
- Advanced Image Prompting (reverse-engineering, master prompts, mockup overlays, AI detection)
- Presentation Wizardry in Google Slides (generating outlines, repairing layouts)
- Advanced Video & Audio Summaries (timestamped YouTube deep-dives, contradiction finding)
- The Planning Partner (event planning, travel itineraries, offsites)
- Brainstorming & Ideation (overcoming writer's block, lateral thinking prompts)
- Quick Research Workspace (Gemini Notebook setup and cross-document synthesis)
- Interactive Study & Prep (Gemini Notebook flashcards and interview simulations)
- Creating Audio Overviews (AI podcasts and real-time interruption)
- End-to-end Capstone project integration

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, participants will be able to:

- Retrieve, summarize, and draft replies to complex email threads directly in Gmail.
- Extract structured data from unstructured text and format it in Google Sheets.
- Use advanced image prompting to reverse-engineer visuals, apply headshots to mockups, and detect AI-generated artifacts.
- Extract advanced insights from YouTube videos, including evaluating rhetorical techniques or finding contradictions.
- Plan comprehensive events and brainstorm creative campaigns using lateral thinking prompts.
- Build and interrogate centralized research workspaces using Gemini Notebook.
- Generate interactive study aids and multimedia audio overviews from Notebook sources.

## Project Context / Tools

The course uses the Gemini web application, Gmail, Google Docs, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Slides, Gemini Notebook, and YouTube. Participants should use non-sensitive material or prepared sample files during the labs. Because all labs are modular and self-contained, participants can jump into any lab without needing the output of previous sessions.

## Day 1: Everyday Productivity & Advanced Media

### 1. Mastering the Inbox
**Concept & Demo:** Introduce how to use Gemini to manage overwhelming email threads. Demonstrate advanced few-shot prompting to extract only action items, summarize decisions, and draft replies that perfectly mimic the user's natural writing tone. 
**Breakout Lab 1.1: Inbox Zero:** Participants search for a real or sample project thread and use Gemini to generate a bulleted executive summary and draft a polite, tone-matched follow-up email.

### 2. Smart Document Creation
**Concept & Demo:** Demonstrate iterative drafting: providing an outline, asking Gemini to draft section by section, and adjusting the tone. Show how to summarize long PDFs by asking targeted, specific questions to avoid generic summaries.
**Breakout Lab 2.1: The Draft Architect:** Participants upload a dense sample PDF (e.g., a policy doc or research paper) and use iterative prompting to extract specific rules or findings into a structured Google Doc.

### 3. Everyday Data Assistant
**Concept & Demo:** Users often need to clean up lists or extract data instead of building complex formulas. Demonstrate how to take unstructured text (like a messy meeting transcript or a list of raw feedback) and instruct Gemini to structure it into a clean, categorized table ready for Google Sheets. 
**Breakout Lab 3.1: Data Wrangler:** Participants copy a block of provided unstructured text (e.g., product reviews or survey responses) and prompt Gemini to extract the core themes, sentiment, and action items into a categorized table.

### 4. Advanced Image Prompting
**Concept & Demo:** Teach advanced visual concepts that go beyond basic generation. Demonstrate how to reverse-engineer an existing image by asking Gemini to describe it in detail, then using that description to create a "master prompt." Show how to generate multiple cohesive images, how to upload a headshot to create a mockup (e.g., a face on a t-shirt or a mug with a company slogan), how to generate an event logo, and how to spot tells of AI-generated images.
**Breakout Lab 4.1: The Visual Reverse-Engineer:** Participants find an appealing image online, reverse-engineer its prompt using Gemini, and tweak the master prompt to generate a cohesive set of event logos or branded mockups.

### 5. Presentation Wizardry
**Concept & Demo:** Show how to quickly draft source-grounded presentations. Discuss how to prompt Gemini for a slide-by-slide narrative structure, generate speaker notes, and use Gemini in Google Slides to repair or improve the layout of a single cluttered slide.
**Breakout Lab 5.1: The Slide Alchemist:** Participants paste an outline or existing notes into Gemini to generate a 5-slide presentation structure, complete with suggested visuals and speaker notes, then practice generating one slide in Google Slides.

### 6. Advanced Video & Audio Summaries
**Concept & Demo:** Demonstrate deep-dive multimodal analysis. Show how to paste a YouTube link and ask Gemini for timestamps, the speaker's rhetorical techniques, missing counter-arguments, or contradictory statements made during the recording. 
**Breakout Lab 6.1: Media Deep Dive:** Participants select a provided YouTube lecture or tutorial, prompt Gemini to find specific timestamps for a niche topic, and ask it to critique the speaker's primary argument.

## Day 2: Planning, Ideation & Research

### 7. The Planning Partner
**Concept & Demo:** Use Gemini to take the mental load off logistics. Demonstrate how to prompt Gemini to plan a comprehensive team offsite, generate a packing list for a trip, or create a detailed hour-by-hour run-of-show for an event, including considering dietary restrictions and budget constraints.
**Breakout Lab 7.1: The Master Planner:** Participants define a hypothetical or upcoming event (e.g., a product launch or family vacation) and generate a comprehensive logistical itinerary and checklist.

### 8. Brainstorming & Ideation
**Concept & Demo:** Show how Gemini can overcome writer's block. Demonstrate lateral thinking prompts (e.g., "Explain this concept as if you were a 1920s detective," or "Give me 10 terrible ideas for this marketing campaign, then make the worst one good"). 
**Breakout Lab 8.1: Out-of-the-Box Ideation:** Participants pick a stagnant project or problem and use a lateral thinking prompt to generate 5 unexpected lateral-thinking solutions.

### 9. Quick Research Workspace (Gemini Notebook)
**Concept & Demo:** Introduce Gemini Notebook as a centralized research hub. Demonstrate how to ingest a handful of diverse materials (PDFs, raw notes, web articles) and ask cross-document questions to get synthesized answers without hallucination. 
**Breakout Lab 9.1: The Knowledge Hub:** Participants create a Notebook project, upload 3 provided sample documents on a single topic, and use the workspace to synthesize a quick summary answering a specific question across all sources.

### 10. Interactive Study & Prep
**Concept & Demo:** Explore how Gemini Notebook can prepare you for high-stakes meetings or exams. Demonstrate how to instantly generate digital study aids (flashcards, quizzes) or simulate a tough Q&A session based purely on the uploaded source material.
**Breakout Lab 10.1: The Prep Simulator:** Participants use their existing Notebook to generate a set of practice questions and simulate an interview or practice Q&A session to test their knowledge.

### 11. Creating Audio Overviews
**Concept & Demo:** Transform text-based research into engaging media. Guide participants in generating a two-host Audio Overview (AI podcast) discussing their source material. Demonstrate how to use the "live interruption" feature to steer the conversation in real-time, focusing the hosts on specific details.
**Breakout Lab 11.1: The Audio Producer:** Participants generate an Audio Overview based on their Notebook sources, practice live interruption to adjust the discussion focus, and save the output.

### 12. Bring Your Own Challenge (Capstone)
**Concept & Demo:** Discuss best practices for integrating Gemini into daily workflows permanently. The instructor helps brainstorm ways to tackle specific participants' real-world blockers.
**Breakout Lab 12.1: End-to-End Application:** Participants independently select a fresh, real-world task relevant to their day-to-day life. They apply any technique from the last two days (master prompting, document synthesis, or planning) to produce a fully independent capstone artifact ready to use.

## Teaching Philosophy

The instructor focuses on advanced, non-obvious techniques (reverse engineering, lateral prompting, multimodal deep-dives) rather than basic generation. Demonstrations are rich but tightly timed to 40 minutes, ensuring participants have a dedicated 20 minutes to practice the technique on their own terms. Every lab is strictly modular, allowing participants to use their own ad-hoc context without needing the output of prior sessions.
