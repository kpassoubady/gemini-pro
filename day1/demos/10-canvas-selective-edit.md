# Demo: Selective Editing in Canvas

## Purpose

Demonstrate how to refine one paragraph while preserving the original argument.

## Prompt

```text
Keep the claim and intended audience unchanged. Make this selected paragraph more precise by removing one cliché and clarifying the missing logical link. Do not add facts that are not in the draft. List the change you made and one question the writer should answer.
```

## Delivery

1. Move a prepared draft into Canvas.
2. Select one paragraph and request the targeted edit.
3. Compare the original and revised versions.
4. Reject or revise any change that alters the intended claim.

## Takeaway

Selective editing supports human authorship when the writer checks meaning, evidence, and voice.
