# Demo: Storyboard-to-Video Handoff

## Purpose

Turn two approved story frames into a bounded motion plan that preserves appearance, geography, and narrative continuity.

## Inputs

Use the approved Frame 2 and Frame 3 keyframes from the Mina and Orbit story. Frame 2 ends with Orbit beside the damaged panel. Frame 3 shows Mina reconnecting one cable while Orbit lights the work area.

## Shot 2 motion prompt

```text
Animate approved Frame 2 for five seconds. Mina remains still and studies the panel. Orbit glides 20 cm left to right while its cyan ring pulses twice, and the scan follows that path across the panel. Use a slow camera push-in with no cut or zoom. Preserve identity, hands, clothing, props, station geometry, dawn lighting, palette, and gouache texture. Add no dialogue, text, logo, person, or object. End with Orbit stationary beside the panel and Mina looking screen-right.
```

## Shot 3 motion prompt

```text
Animate approved Frame 3 for five seconds. Begin with Orbit stationary in the same screen position and Mina looking screen-right. Mina reconnects one visible cable; Orbit holds a steady cyan work light. The station lights turn on once from foreground to background. Use a locked medium camera. Preserve identity, hands, clothing, props, station geometry, palette, and gouache texture. Add no dialogue, text, logo, person, or object. End on a still pose suitable for the next shot.
```

## Handoff checklist

| Signal | End of Shot 2 | Start of Shot 3 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Orbit | Beside panel, stationary | Same position and scale |
| Mina | Looking screen-right | Same eyeline before reaching |
| Light | Cyan ring pulse ends | Cyan work light becomes steady |
| Geography | Close view of panel | Medium view of same panel |

## Delivery

1. Show both approved keyframes and ask learners what must match across the cut.
2. Generate each clip from its keyframe where image-to-video is available.
3. Reject unexpected redesigns, extra objects, reversed geography, or uncontrolled camera motion.
4. Assemble accepted clips in Google Vids; add reviewed narration and captions after the visual cut works.
5. If video generation is unavailable, use the prompts and checklist to review two prepared clips or stills.

## Takeaway

The keyframe controls appearance, the motion prompt controls change over time, and the handoff checklist connects independently generated shots.
