# Demo: Turn Confirmed Tracker Facts into an Announcement

## Purpose

Demonstrate a controlled handoff from a verified Sheet to a concise Google Docs announcement.

## Prompt

Replace the bracketed placeholders with details from your tracker and audience.

```text
Using only the confirmed facts in [PROJECT-TRACKER-NAME], draft an internal announcement for [AUDIENCE]. Include the milestone, owners, current status, next action, and date. Do not invent a deadline or imply approval. Use a concise, professional tone and end with one clear call to action.
```

## Delivery

1. Review the Sheet’s confirmed facts and unresolved items.
2. Generate the draft in a blank Google Doc.
3. Compare every date and owner with the Sheet.
4. Remove unsupported wording and review the audience and call to action.

## Takeaway

Automation should move verified facts between Workspace surfaces, not manufacture certainty.
