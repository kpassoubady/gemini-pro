Use this as an alternate cumulative review after the first afternoon session. It covers connected retrieval, cognitive frameworks, PDF synthesis, and multimodal analysis.

## Q1 (MCQ)

**Question:** What makes generated evidence easier to audit?

A. Source, date, and output fields
B. A conclusion without a source reference
C. A date without the originating source
D. Assumptions presented as evidence

**Correct Answer:** A

## Q2 (True/False)

**Question:** Simulated personas are independent experts.

A. True
B. False

**Correct Answer:** B

## Q3 (MCQ)

**Question:** Why include page numbers in PDF extraction?

A. To make claims traceable
B. To replace sampling against the report
C. To hide missing claims
D. To omit surrounding context

**Correct Answer:** A

**Question:** What should you do with a generated video timestamp?
